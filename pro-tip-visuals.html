Three ways to draw the "same people" tip

Divergent concepts for the Pro tip: interview the same kind of people you see in your data, so the two line up. Each is drawn in the article's sketchbook style and shows the matched case against the mismatched one.

Concept 1 · Inside the segment

Set membership. The big circle is your customer segment. The data group and the interview group are smaller circles inside it. Both inside means both really are your customers; one outside means that group is a different crowd.

Concept 2 · One person, two answers

The literal version. The same person gives you both the number and the reason, so they fit. Ask two different people and the number and the quote can point opposite ways.

Concept 3 · The bridge

A journey metaphor. The "what" and the "why" sit on two banks; the same people are the bridge that lets you cross from one to the other. Different people leave a gap.
