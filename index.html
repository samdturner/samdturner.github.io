I wrote a book

What it is

Honest lessons from ten years of building products, the wins and the misses both. It's packed with real experiences, so you can decide what works for you.

Why I wrote it

Partly because I love sharpening this craft. Mostly so others can start further ahead instead of learning it all the hard way.

Who it's for

It's aimed at B2B SaaS teams, but the method isn't picky. If you're building a product and want to get there faster and with better odds, it's for you.

Books that shaped how I think

The ones I keep coming back to and recommend people most often.

  • The Mom Test

    Rob Fitzpatrick

    How to talk to customers so they tell you the truth instead of being nice.

  • Running Lean

    Ash Maurya

    The step-by-step version of pressure-testing an idea before you build it.

  • Sprint

    Jake Knapp

    Five days from a fuzzy problem to a prototype real customers have tried.

  • Shape Up

    Ryan Singer

    Fixed time, variable scope — the clearest argument for betting in weeks.

  • The Hard Thing About Hard Things

    Ben Horowitz

    The unglamorous half of building a company, from someone who lived it.

  • Crucial Conversations

    Kerry Patterson et al.

    What to do when the stakes are high and the conversation is going badly.

  • Made to Stick

    Chip Heath & Dan Heath

    Why some ideas lodge in people's heads and others slide straight off.

  • 99 Bottles of OOP

    Sandi Metz & Katrina Owen

    One small problem, refactored over and over, until good design is obvious.
