The Product Playbook

The board above is the method I use to take a big, risky idea and turn it into something people pay for. It has two halves: eight steps that de-risk the idea before you build it, and a weekly sprint you run on repeat — one lap a week — until the product lands.

I drew it because most of the advice I found was either strategy with no instructions, or process with no evidence behind it. This is the version I wish someone had handed me: everything I'd actually do, in the order I'd do it, on one page. The steps I've written about link out to the essay; the rest are on their way.
